Privacy Policy

This is the personal portfolio website of Bhaskar Chari. It is a static site: it has no accounts, forms, comments or tracking, and it does not collect personal information on its own. This page explains what does happen when you visit.

What this site does not do

  • No analytics or advertising scripts.
  • No cookies set by this site.
  • No contact form, login or newsletter signup.
  • No data stored in your browser by this site. The search/navigation palette (Ctrl/⌘ K) runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing anywhere.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. As with any web host, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address, browser type and request logs when you load a page. I do not have access to these logs through this site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Fonts

Pages load typefaces (IBM Plex Sans, IBM Plex Mono and Instrument Serif) from Google Fonts. Your browser therefore makes requests to Google servers, which receive your IP address and basic browser information. See the Google Privacy Policy.

Email

If you email me at charibhaskar2608@gmail.com, I receive your email address and whatever you choose to include. I use it only to read and reply to your message, and I do not sell or share it. You can ask me to delete a conversation at any time.

Third-party links

The site links to external services, including GitHub, LinkedIn and IEEE Xplore, and to a resume PDF hosted with the site. Once you follow a link to another service, that service's own privacy policy applies. I am not responsible for their practices.

Children

This site is intended for a general professional audience and is not directed at children.

Changes

If anything on the site changes how data is handled, for example adding analytics, I will update this page and the date above.
